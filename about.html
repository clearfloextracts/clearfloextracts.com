Clear Flo Extracts is a family-run extract cleaning and ventilation hygiene business. We cover Kent and London, we do the work ourselves, and we don’t cut corners.

Who we are

We’re a small family business based in Minster, Thanet. Extract cleaning and ventilation hygiene is all we do — and we’ve been doing it for over nine years. That means you get a team that knows these systems inside out, not a franchise sending out whoever’s available.

We grew up working with our hands. Before starting Clear Flo Extracts, our background was in building, property maintenance, and facilities work — including years on large permit sites at Canary Wharf and London Bridge City. We understand commercial kitchens, the pressures chefs and managers are under, and what a proper TR19® clean actually involves.

When we turn up to a job, the same people who quoted it are the ones who clean it. You’ll always speak to someone who was actually there.

Properly qualified

BESA GHT/GHO trained and NAADUK accredited. We carry the right certifications and we know what TR19® compliance actually requires.

No shortcuts

Every job is photographed before and after and fully documented. The report you receive at the end is one you can hand to your insurer or EHO with confidence.

Local & reliable

✅

NAADUK Accredited

National Association of Air Duct and Hygiene Professionals accreditation — independently verified competence.

✅

TR19® Compliant

9+ Years Experience

Over nine years in ventilation and facilities work, including large commercial sites at Canary Wharf and London Bridge City.
