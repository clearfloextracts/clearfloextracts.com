Why fire damper servicing matters

Fire dampers are passive fire protection devices installed in ductwork to prevent fire and smoke spreading through a building. They are required by law to be inspected and tested regularly — and when they fail, the consequences can be severe.

Under BS 9999, fire dampers must be tested at least annually, with quarterly checks recommended for high-risk environments. A failed or untested damper is not only a life safety risk — it can invalidate your fire insurance and leave you personally liable.

We carry out damper inspections and servicing as a standalone service or as part of a planned maintenance contract. Every visit produces a full written report, test record and remedial recommendations — everything your fire safety file needs.

What can go wrong

  • Damper blades seized open — will not close in a fire event
  • Actuator failure — damper cannot respond to fire alarm signal
  • Grease build-up preventing blade movement
  • Missing or damaged access panels preventing inspection
  • No test record — compliance gap for insurance and EHO
  • Dampers installed but never commissioned or tested
